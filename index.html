I'm very nervous about driving. Can you help?

Yes. Many of Sujan's students start out anxious. Lessons go at your pace, start on quiet streets, and build up only when you feel ready.

How do I convert my overseas licence in Tasmania?

It depends on where your licence is from. Licences from recognised countries can usually be converted at Service Tasmania without a test. Licences from other countries generally need a knowledge test, an online Hazard Perception Test and a practical driving assessment. Drivers under 25 from experienced-driver-recognition countries also sit these tests. Check your country on the Transport Tasmania website, then book lessons to prepare for the practical assessment.
