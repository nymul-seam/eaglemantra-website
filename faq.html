Questions & answers

Everything you need to know about learning to drive with Eagle Driving Mantra.

How much are driving lessons?

A 50-minute lesson is $70 and a 60-minute lesson is $75. Call or text 0432 435 996 to book.

Which areas do you cover?

Eagle Driving Mantra is based in Glenorchy and teaches across Greater Hobart, including Moonah, New Town, the Hobart CBD, Sandy Bay, Kingston and the Eastern Shore. Call to check pickup from your suburb.

I'm very nervous about driving. Can you help?

Yes. Many of Sujan's students start out anxious. Lessons go at your pace, start on quiet streets, and build up only when you feel ready.

How do I convert my overseas licence in Tasmania?

It depends on where your licence is from. Licences from recognised countries can usually be converted at Service Tasmania without a test. Licences from other countries generally need a knowledge test, an online Hazard Perception Test and a practical driving assessment. Drivers under 25 from experienced-driver-recognition countries also sit these tests. Check your country on the Transport Tasmania website, then book lessons to prepare for the practical assessment.

What do I need before my P1 driving assessment?

For most new learners in Tasmania you must be at least 17, have held your learner licence for 12 months, logged 80 supervised hours including 15 at night, and passed the Hazard Perception Test. Requirements can differ if you got your learner licence before December 2020, so check Transport Tasmania's website.

How many lessons will I need?

It depends on your experience. Overseas licence holders often need only a few lessons to learn local rules and test expectations, while complete beginners usually need more. After your first lesson Sujan will give you an honest recommendation.

Do you offer mock driving tests?

Yes. P1 test preparation includes a mock test run like the real assessment, followed by clear feedback on what to practise.

How do I book a lesson?

Call or text 0432 435 996, message on WhatsApp, or email hello@eaglemantra.com.au. You can also use the enquiry form.

Can I drive my own car in lessons?

Lessons are in the instructor's dual-control car, which is safer while you learn. Ask Sujan if you'd like to practise in your own car too.

What should I bring to my first lesson?

Bring your current learner or overseas licence, glasses or contacts if you need them, and comfortable flat shoes.
