Your road to a Tasmanian licence

Here's the usual path for new drivers in Tasmania. Lessons can help at every stop.

L

Learner (L1 → L2)

Pass the knowledge test and start learning with a supervisor. Log your hours in your logbook.

L

Get test-ready

Hold your learner licence for 12 months, log 80 hours (15 at night) and pass the Hazard Perception Test.

P

P1 assessment

Pass the practical driving assessment to drive solo on red P plates.

P

P2, then full licence

Keep a clean record through the provisional stages to earn your full licence.

Summary of the current pathway for most new learners. Always check the latest rules on the Transport Tasmania website.
