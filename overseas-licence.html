Overseas licence conversion in Hobart

New to Tasmania? Learn the local road rules and get ready for your conversion driving assessment with an instructor who's helped hundreds of overseas drivers.

How licence conversion works in Tasmania

What you need depends on the country that issued your licence.

Recognised countries

Usually no test

If your current licence is from a recognised country, you can usually convert it at Service Tasmania without a test.

Experienced driver countries

Depends on your age

If you're under 25, you'll need a knowledge test, the online Hazard Perception Test and a conversion driving assessment.

All other countries

Tests required

You'll need to pass a knowledge test, the Hazard Perception Test and a practical driving assessment.

Rules can change. Check your country and the latest requirements on the Transport Tasmania website.
